Privacy Policy.

At AURA, we respect your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be handled when you use our website and tools.

1. Information We Collect

AURA is designed to provide online tools with a simple user experience. Depending on the features you use, the website may process information that you voluntarily provide.

2. Files You Upload

Some AURA tools may allow you to upload files for processing. We aim to process files only as necessary to provide the requested tool.

Do not upload confidential, sensitive or highly personal information unless you are comfortable using the particular tool.

3. Cookies

AURA may use cookies or similar technologies in the future to improve website functionality, understand usage and support features such as analytics or advertising.

4. Analytics

We may use analytics services in the future to understand how visitors use AURA, such as which pages or tools are used most frequently.

5. Advertising

AURA may display advertisements in the future. Advertising partners may use technologies such as cookies to provide or measure relevant ads, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable laws.

6. Third-Party Services

Some AURA features may use third-party services. These services may have their own terms and privacy policies. We recommend reviewing those policies when applicable.

7. Security

We take reasonable steps to help protect information associated with AURA. However, no website or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

AURA is not intentionally designed to collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the website, please contact us.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when AURA's features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact AURA through our Contact page.
